## Event-triggered obligations (not dated yet)

- **First dividend paid to Harry** → register for Self Assessment that tax
  year (dividends above the allowance are untaxed income; salary at employer
  stays PAYE and is irrelevant to this).
- **First customer whose personal data we hold** (names, emails, billing —
  pilot signature counts) → pay the ICO data protection fee (Tier 1, ~£52/yr).
  Prompts never touching our machines does not exempt the company: contact and
  billing data is personal data.
- **First paying pilot** → professional indemnity + cyber insurance quotes
  (not legally required with no employees, but FLUX sits in the customer's API
  request path and B2B contracts commonly demand PI).
- **Hiring anyone** → employers' liability insurance becomes a legal duty
  same-day, PAYE registration needed. Currently: no employees.
- **Turnover approaching £90k in any rolling 12 months** → VAT registration
  becomes mandatory. Voluntary registration before then: deliberately not
  taken (admin outweighs reclaimable input VAT at current scale) — revisit at
  first revenue.
